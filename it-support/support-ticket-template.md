## Evidence checklist

- Capture the starting symptom and relevant system context.
- Record exact commands, settings reviewed, and the results that support a conclusion.
- Record before/after states for any permitted configuration change.
- Distinguish observations from assumptions; protect credentials and personal details.
- Verify the resolution from the user's perspective when possible; otherwise document the escalation and open questions.
